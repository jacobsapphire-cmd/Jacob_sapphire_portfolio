About Me

I am Jacob Sapphire J C, a GIS Analyst & AI Developer with hands-on experience in spatial analysis, morphometric studies, data visualization, and automation using AI and Python.

I work with ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, Google Earth Pro, Blender 3D, and Python (Flask) to transform geospatial data into actionable insights, interactive dashboards, and web applications.

My PG project — GIS-Based Morphometric Analysis of Agniyar River Basin — involved watershed delineation, hydrology layer extraction, and the first 3D Blender terrain visualization in the PG Geography programme at University of Madras.
